For Minecraft 26.3

This data pack adds the Heliblocker; a helicopter made out of blockerlockers. It can be reskinned to any other player by using a command.

The Heliblocker is crafted with one Pufferfish and one Ghast Harness. When placed, you can right click in the middle to ride it, and then use WASD to rotate and move, Space Bar to fly up, and Sprint to fly down. Punch it a few times quickly to break it.

Run `/function heliblocker:reskin {username:"your_name_here"}` to change the nearest Heliblocker to your skin.
